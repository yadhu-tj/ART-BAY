ART&BAY

Est. 2024 • Kochi, India

The Spark

It began with a frustration. Too many talented artists in India had no platform to showcase their work globally without losing 50% to galleries.

The Build

We assembled a team of developers and curators. The goal was simple: Create a digital space that feels as premium as a physical gallery.

The Launch

In 2025, ART&BAY went live. We introduced "Smart Escrow" and "Direct-to-Artist" messaging, revolutionizing how art is sold online.
